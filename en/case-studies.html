02 — Knowing what to look at

Three things to look at in a before / after

Plenty of comparisons circulate online where the after is simply brighter than the before. That is not a result, it is a camera setting. Here is what actually proves the work was done.

  1. The reflection

    On a resurfaced floor, the reflection of a window is sharp and continuous. On a worn one it is blurred and broken up in patches. That is the most reliable signal, and the hardest to fake.

  2. The walking lines

    Wear follows the routes, it is never uniform. A credible before shows heavier degradation along the walking line than at the foot of the walls.

  3. The framing

    Same angle, same height, same light. If the framing shifts between the two images, the comparison is worthless. Every one on this page is aligned to the pixel.

Every material fails in its own way

Marble scratches, travertine greens, terracotta saturates, porcelain keeps its tiles intact but loses its grout. Recognising how a floor fails is already knowing what to do about it.
