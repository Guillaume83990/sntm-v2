Privacy policy

Document to be completed with the company's legal details before going live.

Data controller

SAP Service Accueil Prestation (SNTM SAP), whose registered office is at Domaine du Château Martin, 1 lotissement Château Martin, 83580 Gassin, France, is the controller of the data collected through this website. Contact: sntm@hotmail.fr.

Data collected

The quote request form collects the information you enter: type of premises, requirement, town, approximate area, floor material, preferred time slot, name, company, phone, email, message and, where applicable, any photos you attach.

No other processing takes place. The site uses no advertising cookies, carries out no profiling and embeds no third-party analytics service.

Purpose

This data is used solely to handle your request, prepare the on-site visit and draw up a quote. It is never sold, rented or passed to third parties for commercial purposes.

Legal basis

Processing is based on your consent, obtained explicitly when the form is submitted, and on the company's legitimate interest in responding to a business enquiry.

Retention period

Enquiries that lead to nothing are kept for three years from the last contact. Data linked to a contract is kept for the duration of the business relationship and the applicable statutory periods.

Recipients

The data is accessible to the company and to its hosting provider, GitHub, Inc. for the preview version, strictly for the purpose of providing the service.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data. To exercise it, write to sntm@hotmail.fr. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority.

Cookies

This website sets no analytics or advertising cookies. Only cookies strictly necessary for the page to function may be used, and those require no prior consent.
