01 — Our principles

Four choices we stand by

These are not values on a wall, they are operating rules. You can check them against a quote and against a job.

01

The floor before anything else

A cleaning company that cannot tell travertine from an encaustic tile ends up damaging what it maintains. That is why floor treatment sits alongside routine maintenance here, rather than as a side service.

02

Diagnosis before the quote

A price given over the phone is a price that will be revised. We come and look, we identify the material and its real condition, and we run a test on a hidden area when the job calls for it. The quote comes afterwards, in writing, area by area.

03

Your calendar, not ours

A shop is cleaned before opening. A school between the last pupil out and the first one in. A site before the keys change hands. The time slot is part of the contract, exactly like the scope, and it is written down.

04

What we don't do

We don't work to a blind fixed price, we don't promise a result without having seen the floor, and we don't take on a job whose material we haven't mastered. Saying no is part of the trade.

Turning down a job whose material you haven't mastered is still the surest way not to ruin it.

Working principle · SNTM SAP

02 — Our territory

The Gulf is not an area like any other

Being local here is not a proximity argument. It is a condition of practice: two constraints found nowhere else in the Var change the way you work.

The old town

The lanes of central Saint-Tropez take neither a large vehicle nor a long stay. You work there early, on foot from the drop-off point, with what you can carry. That is planned, not improvised.

The coast

Salt attacks porous surfaces, and the sand carried in from the beaches sands the floors down with every footstep. Two factors you won't meet thirty kilometres inland, and they change the maintenance rhythm.

03 — Recognising us

You'll see us coming

No chain of subcontractors, no operative you meet for the first time that morning. The same van, the same people, one contact you call directly.

04 — Where we work

The Gulf of Saint-Tropez, and the wider Var depending on the nature and length of the job. If your town is not listed, just ask.
