The shopfront

Four square metres decide the rest

A customer forms a view of a shop before setting foot in it. That view turns on a window, a threshold and three metres of floor. They are also the hardest-worked surfaces, and the hardest to keep in season.

The window

Seen from the street against the light, an acceptable pane becomes a dirty one. It is the only surface of the shop a customer judges before entering.

The threshold and the doormat

Where sand and water collect. A neglected threshold recontaminates the sales floor with every step.

The entrance area

The first three metres take as much footfall as the whole of the rest of the shop combined. They wear first, and they are visible from the street.

Fingermarks

On handles, counter cabinets and glass display units. Invisible head-on, obvious in raking light.

Time slots

We come when you are closed

A shop is not cleaned in front of the customer. Four slots are possible, and your business decides which, not our schedule.

01

Before opening

The most common visit. The premises are made ready to receive, window included, before the first customer pushes the door.

02

During the midday closing

For shops that close at lunchtime, a short pass is enough to reset the entrance floor at the height of the season.

03

After closing

The most comfortable slot for longer work, and the only option for shops that stay open all day.

04

On the weekly closing day

The window for heavy work: floor treatment, high-level glazing, a full reset after a busy week.

The season

August has nothing in common with February

On the Gulf, a shop takes a full year's footfall in four months. On top of that comes the sand carried in from the beaches, acting as an abrasive on the entrance floor with every step.

A contract with the same rhythm across twelve months is badly designed: too expensive in winter, not enough in summer. The frequency varies, and that variation is written into the contract.

Before you open

Eight things to check every morning

Whether we come or you do, these are the eight things a customer notices. Use them as a checklist, including to judge us.

  2. Threshold clear, doormat shaken out or replaced

The floor

The line item that costs most to neglect

A retail floor takes the sand, the water, the salt and the footfall of an entire season. If it is natural stone, terracotta or encaustic tile, the wrong maintenance product damages it lastingly, and restoration costs several times the price of upkeep.

The floor material is therefore identified at the visit, before the quote. It dictates the products, not the other way round.

Frequently asked questions

What shopkeepers ask

Do you come before the shop opens?

Yes, that is the most common arrangement. The slot is set in the contract, based on your opening time and the time actually needed. It is written down, alongside the areas covered and the frequency.

Can the frequency change between summer and winter?

It must. On the Gulf, a shop takes footfall between June and September on a scale unmatched by the rest of the year. A contract with the same rhythm across twelve months is either too expensive in winter or not enough in summer.

Is the window included?

It is part of the scope to be defined explicitly. Inside glazing, outside glazing reachable from ground level, frames and handles do not involve the same work or the same time: they are priced separately.

What if my floor is stone or terracotta?

It changes everything. Marble, travertine and terracotta are not washed with the same product as porcelain, and the wrong one damages them lastingly. The material is identified at the visit, before the quote.

How long does a visit take?

It depends on the area, the floor material, the number of washrooms and the footfall, far more than on square metres alone. The duration is established after a visit, not over the phone.

Do you work beyond central Saint-Tropez?

Yes. We cover the whole Gulf, including Ramatuelle, Gassin, Grimaud, Cogolin, Sainte-Maxime, Cavalaire-sur-Mer and La Croix-Valmer, plus other towns in the Var depending on the contract.
